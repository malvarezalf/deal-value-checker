A deal can create value overall and still destroy it for the buyer, if the premium hands more than the net synergies to the seller.

Fix your maximum price before negotiating. Deal fever and agency incentives push bids up once the team is committed.

Trade beyond price

If the price is too high, move value into non-price terms: earn-outs, stock consideration, governance or retention, which cost the buyer less.
